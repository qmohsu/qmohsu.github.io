The PolyU Research Institute for Artificial Intelligence of Things (RIAIoT) awarded **HK$800,000** to *Robust Seamless Vehicle Positioning Across Road-to-Parking Transitions via Multimodal Sensor Fusion*, led by **Li-Ta Hsu as PI**. The award was approved on **21 September 2026**.

The project investigates how multimodal sensor fusion can maintain reliable vehicle positioning through open roads, entrance zones, ramps and covered parking environments. It aims to develop a positioning workflow and evaluation approach for these transitions.
