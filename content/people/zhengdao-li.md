Zhengdao Li is a PhD student at IPNL, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, supervised by Prof. Li-Ta Hsu. His research examines GNSS errors with non-Gaussian distributions, robust statistical approaches to positioning in cities, and navigation integrity for safety-critical applications.
