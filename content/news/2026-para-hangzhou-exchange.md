On 28 September 2026, Prof. Li-Ta Hsu presented IPNL's research during an exchange through the **PolyU Aerospace Research Academy (PARA)** with a visiting delegation from **杭州市实业投资集团** in Hangzhou.

The presentation introduced the laboratory's work on satellite navigation and positioning in urban environments, alongside examples of research collaboration with industry. The exchange provided an opportunity to discuss how positioning research can connect with practical applications.
