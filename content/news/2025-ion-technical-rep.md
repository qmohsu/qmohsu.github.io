Prof. Li-Ta Hsu was elected as a **Technical Representative on the Institute of Navigation (ION) Council** for a two-year term beginning **30 January 2025**. The election is recorded in the [ION Winter 2025 newsletter, page 26](https://www.ion.org/newsletter/upload/ION-Winter2025.pdf#page=26).

He previously served a separate two-year term beginning **31 January 2019**, documented in the [ION Winter 2019 newsletter, page 18](https://www.ion.org/publications/upload/v29n1.pdf#page=18). The two terms are **2019–2021 and 2025–2027**.
