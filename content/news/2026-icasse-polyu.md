The **10th International Conference on Aerospace System Science and Engineering (ICASSE 2026)** took place at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University on **16–17 July 2026**. Shanghai Jiao Tong University organised the conference, with PolyU's Department of Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering hosting it.

**Prof. Li-Ta Hsu** and **Prof. Wu Xu** served as **General Chairs**, as listed by the [conference committee](https://icasse.sjtu.edu.cn/Data/List/Thenamelistofconferencecommittee). The programme connected researchers and industry participants working on topics including satellite navigation, autonomous flight and AI-enabled aerospace systems.

PolyU's [Research and Innovation Office retrospective](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/polyu-rio-icasse2026-ugcPost-7487408971591581696-OXQy) also covered the completed conference and its international academic exchanges.
