Prof. **Li-Ta Hsu** received the Institute of Navigation's **Red Pencil Recognition** for a second time. The recognition was announced at the ION conference in **September 2026**.

The recognition acknowledges contributions to peer review across ION activities. ION describes its purpose in the [Winter 2026 newsletter, page 13](https://www.ion.org/newsletter/upload/ION-Winter2026.pdf#page=13); that article reports the previous year's recipients.
