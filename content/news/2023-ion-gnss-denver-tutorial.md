Prof. Li-Ta Hsu contributed to **[ION GNSS+ 2023](https://www.ion.org/gnss/)** in Denver, Colorado in September 2023 in two service roles:

**Session Chair** and **Technical Track Chair (Track E)** at the conference — continuing recurring contributions across ION GNSS+ editions.

ION GNSS+ is widely regarded as the premier annual gathering for the global GNSS technical community, with hundreds of paper presentations across parallel tracks each year.

The [official programme, page 2](https://www.ion.org/publications/upload/GNSS23-Onsite-Program.pdf#page=2) lists Prof. Hsu as the Technical Track Chair for Track E.

The planned indoor-navigation tutorial was cancelled, as marked in the [official programme, page 7](https://www.ion.org/publications/upload/GNSS23-Onsite-Program.pdf#page=7).
