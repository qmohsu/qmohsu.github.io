**Prof. Li-Ta Hsu**, from PolyU's Department of Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering, serves as a Guest Editor for the **Intelligent urban computing** Collection in *Scientific Reports*.

The Collection welcomes original research on urban sensing and multi-sensor fusion, connecting real-world applications with urban perception and situational awareness.

**Submission deadline: 22 June 2027.**

Researchers are invited to consult the [official Collection page](https://www.nature.com/collections/gfaecbebdc) for its scope and submission guidance. The publisher also provides [information about the Guest Editors](https://www.nature.com/collections/gfaecbebdc/guest-editors).
