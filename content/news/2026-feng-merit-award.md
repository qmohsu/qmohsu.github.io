**Prof. Li-Ta Hsu** has been named a **Merit Award** recipient in the **Research and Scholarly Activities: Outstanding Early Career Researcher** category of the **Faculty Awards for Outstanding Achievement 2026** at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University.

The Faculty of Engineering announced the results on **15 September 2026**. The award recognises Hsu, an Associate Professor in the Department of Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering, in the Faculty's annual research and scholarly activities awards.

The Faculty's prize presentation ceremony is scheduled for **February or March 2027**.
