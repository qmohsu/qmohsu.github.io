*Official announcement artwork: Department of Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering, The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. The artwork identifies the 2026 edition (version 9).*

Li-Ta Hsu is included in both the **Career-Long** and **Single-Year** lists of the Stanford–Elsevier World's Top 2% Scientists, **2026 edition**. PolyU's Department of Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering [announced the recognition on 7 October 2026](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/aae/news-and-events/news/2026/20261007_aae-scholars-2026/?sc_lang=en), naming Hsu among eleven AAE scholars.

The [official dataset, version 9](https://elsevier.digitalcommonsdata.com/datasets/btchxktzyw/9) was published on 7 October 2026. Its Career-Long indicators cover citations through the end of **2025**; the Single-Year indicators cover citations received during **2025**. The edition year is therefore 2026, while the latest citation year is 2025.
